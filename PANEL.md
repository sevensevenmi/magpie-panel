# Magpie Maintenance Panel

This is an unofficial public maintenance fork of
[`yetone/magpie`](https://github.com/yetone/magpie). Upstream remains the
source of truth for the application, releases, provider contracts and
production installs.

## Scope

- keep `main` as a fast-forward mirror of upstream `main`;
- use `panel` for public issue/PR triage, reproducible fixes and local patches;
- enumerate upstream issues and pull requests with evidence and status;
- propose focused fixes upstream before carrying them in this fork;
- never publish upstream packages, releases or distribution channels.

## Safety

Do not post cookies, tokens, JWTs, account identifiers, private prompts or
unredacted local paths. This fork does not claim upstream collaborator
permissions.
